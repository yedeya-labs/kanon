# The Kanon name

The code and the rulebook are licensed under Apache-2.0, which lets you use, change and redistribute them. The licence does **not** grant use of the name "Kanon" (Apache-2.0 §6).

- **You may** say that your project *uses* Kanon, *follows* Kanon, or is *based on* Kanon.
- **You may not** call a modified version, a fork or a hosted service "Kanon", or present it as the official Kanon, without written permission.
- **If you fork Kanon and change its rules,** give your fork a different name. Kanon's value is that its opinions mean the same thing in every repository that adopts them.

Questions about the name: open an issue, or contact the maintainer.
